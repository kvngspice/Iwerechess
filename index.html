The set

A kingdom you can play

Each piece takes its form from something the Itsekiri people already honour: the Olu’s crown, the chiefs’ regalia, the Red Boat and the masquerade. The board is the water of Àlè Ìwẹ̀rẹ̀, with the rivers of the kingdom etched faintly beneath the squares.
